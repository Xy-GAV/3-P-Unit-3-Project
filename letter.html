Dearest Alexander

You’ve told me you appreciate my poeticism, yet here I sit, flustered and bothered writing of the sweet things you’ve done to me. It’s pathetic. I’ve been burned in all that is you and I cannot flee, but I don’t want to. You’re odd
Cal.

Dearest Alexander

I know you’ll think it silly, time it’s been since we’ve last had a moment to talk. I’d love if you showed up at the estate for tea. Any time of day or night. I’ll awake for your company.
Please. I’ve missed you. Cal

Dearest Alexander.

I hate to report to you with unfavorable news, but it seems I must away soon. Sherman has requested me and I may not say no. Await me when I return?
Cal.
